Voltage drop & cable sizing

Long, undersized runs lose voltage. Keep the drop under 3% for critical gear. Estimate it with the app’s calculator, then size up a gauge if needed.

Battery life

Most marine batteries last 4–5 years. Write the install date on the battery and log it here — age is the №1 cause of “dies at the dock”.
