Logging

Viewing your logbook

Browse your full history in one place.

Steps

  1. Open the Log tab to see all entries newest-first.
  2. Filter by type — trips, fills, services, notes.
  3. Tap an entry to see details.
  4. Export the logbook as a PDF for a copy.

Tips

  • The logbook rides along in your data backup.
  • Use it to spot patterns ahead of the season.
