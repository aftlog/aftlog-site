Adding engine hours

Record engine hours so intervals and planning stay accurate.

Steps

  1. Open your boat and go to the Log tab.
  2. Tap to add hours with the current odometer or hour-meter reading.
  3. AftLog stores the total and keeps it current.
  4. Update it whenever you run the engine.

Tips

  • Total hours drive interval due-dates and the fuel-range brain.
  • Start a GPS trip to log hours automatically.
