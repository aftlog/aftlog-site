Adding documents

Attach manuals, receipts, and registration to your boat.

Steps

  1. Open the boat hub and go to Documents.
  2. Tap Add and pick a photo or file.
  3. Add a name and expiry date if it applies.
  4. Save. Expiring documents get reminders.

Tips

  • Add your engine manual here to power Manual extraction.
  • Useful for insurance, registration, and receipts.
