Aug 14, 2026

Diagnostic Assistant Report — the friendly version of the "why"

When something acts up (engine won't start, overheating, vibration), AftLog now writes you a plain-language report instead of just handing you a list:

  • What this usually means — plain language, no jargon
  • What your logs show — tied to your actual trips, fills, and hours
  • Most likely causes, what you can check yourself, and what a mechanic will look at
  • Things to watch for, plus a note on any photos you've attached

It's a summary you can read on the water or hand to a mechanic. A printable PDF version is next.

Aug 10, 2026 — end of day

The full app structure is built

Big day. Beyond the site, the app itself now has its complete structure, working offline on a phone:

  • Boat hub: profile + photo, engine details, documents with expiry, parts, photo timeline, service history, maintenance intervals, cost-of-ownership, resale report PDF, logbook PDF
  • Fuel-range brain: set your tank size, hit Fill up, track trips with GPS or odometer — AftLog learns your real km/L and tells you how far you can go
  • 13 tools: reminders, AI assistant, symptom decoder, ramp mode, calculators, buying advisor, winterization planner, float plan, compliance, manual finder, DIY repairs, battery & electronics, glossary
  • Checklists for launch, retrieve, towing, first outing, spring prep, winterization, used-boat inspection
  • Free tier + $29 one-time Pro, 5 languages, metric/imperial, backup & restore

Next: refining with real boat content and finishing the polish.
