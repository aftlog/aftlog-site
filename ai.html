How It Works

The AI features run through the AftLog portal server — no AI keys are ever in the app. This web demo answers from built-in guidance so it works anywhere; the app's full Ask AftLog is boat-aware (your boat, engine hours, overdue services) and falls back to on-device guidance when you're offline.

  • Server-side AI processing
  • No keys in app or website
  • Secure proxy architecture
  • On-device guidance when offline

Privacy & Offline Mode

AftLog works without a signal. Your logs, photos, manuals, and boat data stay on your device. AI features only reach the server when you choose to ask a question or upload a photo.

  • Works without a signal
  • On-device storage
  • No cloud dependency
  • Explicit, user-triggered AI calls
