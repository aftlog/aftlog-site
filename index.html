How it works

Log offline, export when you're ready, see the big picture

AftLog doesn't force you into an account or a cloud. You use it offline like a proper logbook — and when you want analytics, you send a bundle to the Web Portal.

Step 01

Log your boat offline

Engine hours, fuel, services, checklists, and symptoms — all on your phone, even with no signal at the lake. Reminders fire locally; nothing depends on a server.

Step 02

Export your AftLog bundle

When you want a deeper view, you export a single bundle file from the app. It contains your boats, trips, services, intervals, and checklists — nothing is sent on its own.

Step 03

Send it to the Web Portal

Import the bundle into the AftLog Web Portal to see Year-in-Review, trip analytics, maintenance dashboards, and boat health — all computed from your own log.

FAQ

Questions boat owners actually ask

If you're the kind of person who keeps a notebook in the glove box, this is for you. AftLog is built to replace that notebook — without losing the parts that make it yours.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The app is offline-first: logging, checklists, and reminders all work without signal. AI diagnostics have an offline fallback when you're out of range. Sync and export happen when you choose.

Do I need an account?

No. AftLog does not require an account to use the app. The Web Portal uses your exported bundle, not a live account connection.

Can I track more than one boat?

Yes. Your Pro license covers unlimited boats and engines. Each boat gets its own health score, checklists, and analytics.

Does it work for outboards and inboards?

Yes. AftLog supports common outboard and inboard configurations, with service reminders and checklists tailored to your engine type.

What happens to my data?

Your log lives on your device. You can export it to CSV or PDF anytime. The Web Portal computes analytics from your bundle; we don't sell or mine your data.

Is this available outside Canada and the US?

AftLog is built for boat owners in Canada and the US first, with seasons and reminders tuned to those regions. Other regions may work, but aren't optimized yet.
