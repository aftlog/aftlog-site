New to boating?

You're not supposed to know all this yet — that's the point

Most boating apps assume you've been doing this for years. AftLog doesn't. If your boat is new to you, it teaches the essentials, keeps you safe at the ramp, and only ever shows the one thing you should do next.

Boat Health Score

Is your boat ready? One glance gives you green, yellow, or red — and the single next thing to do. No maintenance expertise required.

Emergency: "What to do if…"

Engine won't start? Taking on water? One tap gets calm, step-by-step instructions — and your exact GPS position ready to share with help.

"Know Your Boat" in 2 minutes

Short guided cards teach the essentials: bilge pump, battery, oil, trim, navigation lights, shallow water. "Got it" moves you on — "show me later" saves it.

Pre-departure checklist

Drain plug, battery, fuel, life jackets, kill switch — every item with a picture of what to look for, so the mistakes everyone makes once, you don't make.

First-time launch helper

Backing up, ramp etiquette, avoiding the jackknife, getting on and off the trailer — guided steps for the most stressful 20 minutes in boating.

Beginner Mode

A simplified dashboard: one next task, your fuel range, and a daily tip. When you're ready for everything, switch to full mode — nothing is locked away.

Features

Built like a good boat: nothing fancy that breaks

Offline-first, like you are at the lake. Every feature below works with zero signal, and syncs when you're back on the dock.

Engine hours & fuel log

One tap to log hours at the dock. Auto-estimates hours from trips if you forget, so the log stays honest.

Service reminders

"Lower-unit oil due in 40 hours." "Winterize in 3 weeks." Based on your engine, your hours, and your region's season.

Seasonal checklists

Commissioning in spring, winterization in fall — prebuilt checklists for your engine type, with photos and receipts attached.

AI diagnostics

"Outboard won't idle after winter storage." Describes symptoms, gets likely causes ranked by what your log shows, in plain language.

Sync, export, back up

Offline-first on device. Sync across phone and tablet when you choose. Export the whole log to PDF or CSV — yours forever.

Symptom decoder

"Why won't it start?" Pick your engine and the symptom — get ranked causes, fix steps, and when to call a mechanic.
